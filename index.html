03 · Projects

Tecdia · In production

AI Visual Inspection System

An AI second opinion for a chip inspection machine. It reads four lighting views of each chip face, decides good or defective, and writes the verdict to the machine's PLC before the pick arm moves.

// problem

In our test the machine's rule-based check let 53% of defective chips through, and any AI verdict had to arrive within the machine's cycle, with triggers a few hundred milliseconds apart.

// what I built

A multi-view EfficientNet with attention fusion, trained on 27.7K chip faces; an asyncio core with a safety interlock that never writes a verdict to the wrong chip; and the data and retraining pipeline behind it.

// result

Head to head on 921 operator-labelled chips the AI won on every headline number, at a median of 156 ms from trigger to verdict. On unseen chips, the new model catches 91% of defects, up from 82%.
